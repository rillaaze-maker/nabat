نمونه‌ی قلم‌ها

آزمون آیه Quran text test: Amiri Quran is the reference. Hollow boxes or loose marks mean a sign is missing.

Hafez and the other fonts render the basic vowel marks correctly, but they lack the Uthmani signs: the small high meem (ۢ) for iqlab, the small waw and yeh (ۥ ۦ), the pause marks (ۛ ۖ ۚ), the Uthmani sukun shape (ۡ) and the end-of-ayah sign (۝). So they can show the Quran in the simple (imla'i) spelling, but not the Uthmani mushaf spelling a hafiz memorizes from.
